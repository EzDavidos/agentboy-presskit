3-4 # AGENTBOY — Artist Bio

Hi, I’m AGENTBOY, my real name is Andrii Smoliar, and I’m originally from Kyiv, Ukraine. I grew up playing sports and never imagined that one day I would find myself in music and DJing.

My journey started when I was 18. I went to a club with my friends and was fascinated by the way the DJ could control the energy of the crowd through music. I stood next to the DJ booth, watching everything he did, and after the party I kept thinking about it. Still, I told myself that becoming a DJ was unrealistic, so I continued simply going to parties and enjoying the music.

One day, while hanging out with friends at a bar, one of them told me, *“Andrii, you should give it a try. If you don’t do it now, you may never get the chance.”* He invited me behind the decks, and we played together. Seeing people enjoy the music and feeling that connection with the crowd completely changed everything for me. I received dozens of great reactions and realized that this was something I truly wanted to pursue.

In 2022, I moved to Greece with my wife and bought my first DJ controller — the Pioneer DDJ-400. From that moment, I spent days and nights practicing, experimenting, and searching for my own sound. I also started learning music production in Ableton Live 11.

The journey was not easy. I visited clubs and bars across Greece, watched different parties, studied the scene, and dreamed of getting my chance to play. Eventually, I was invited to perform at MARMELDA in Komotini, Greece.

I was in a new country, practically unknown to everyone, and more than 50 people came to the event. I was extremely nervous, but once I started playing, I completely immersed myself in the music. The night was a success, and I received amazing feedback from the crowd.

That moment gave me the confidence to believe in myself. I started playing every week, then twice a week, and eventually began focusing more seriously on music production.

After releasing my first original track, which received radio support in Greece and India, I began receiving invitations to perform at different venues across Greece.

Today, my sound is shaped by Progressive House, Indie Dance, and Melodic Techno — music focused on atmosphere, emotion, energy, and creating a strong connection with the crowd.

5. # What Sets Me Apart

My biggest strength is my ability to feel the crowd and read the energy of the dance floor. During my performances, I don’t just focus on the music — I watch the people, their eyes, their movements, and their reactions. This allows me to understand what the crowd needs and create the right energy at the right moment.

I love turning even the most relaxed guests into part of the show. Someone might be sitting at a table, but little by little, the music makes them move — first their foot, then their head, and before they know it, they are dancing with everyone else.

If someone doesn’t know how to move to electronic music, I’m happy to invite them behind the DJ booth and show them the rhythm, explain how the music works, and help them feel the beat from the inside.

For me, a DJ is not just someone standing behind the decks. A DJ is someone who creates emotions, connects people, and brings them together through music.

I’m an open and approachable person, and I always try to connect with the crowd during my performances. I talk to guests, react to their energy, and I’m not afraid to become part of the party myself.

For me, it’s not enough to simply play a good set. I want every person to leave feeling that they were part of something special.
